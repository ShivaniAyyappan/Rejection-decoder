Rejection Decoder

Got a scholarship notice that says "defective" or "rejected" and no clear reason? Paste it here to see what it means, what to fix, and who to contact.

Samples are made-up examples, not real applications.

Do not paste your Aadhaar number, bank account number or phone number.

This tool explains notices using a small library of reasons that was checked by hand. It is not legal advice and it can be wrong or out of date. Always confirm on the official portal before you act.
